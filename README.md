# 🇮🇹 Project Portfolio Documentation

## Executive Summary: The Airbnb Effect on Italian Housing Affordability
* **Project Status:** Completed October 2026 [context]
* **Tools Used:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), Advanced Excel
* **Data Core:** 1,500 urban rental records across Rome, Milan, and Florence (modeled against Inside Airbnb distribution metrics).

### 📌 Project Overview
This project evaluates the intersection of short-term holiday rentals and urban housing affordability in Italy's primary metropolitan areas. As tourism volumes break historical records, local residents face rising rent escalation and diminished long-term inventory. This analysis isolates pricing distributions, quantifies the presence of commercial "multi-hosts," and maps market saturation by property type.

---

### 📊 Core Analytical Findings

#### 1. Rental Premium & Estimated Annual Revenue by City
Grouping the structured dataset by metropolitan area revealed that Milan leads the market with the highest average rental premium, followed closely by Rome and Florence. 

#### 2. Commercial Multi-Host Domination
A critical finding emerged when evaluating market concentration: a significant percentage of active listings are controlled by commercial multi-hosts (entities managing more than 5 distinct properties). The scatter plot tracking Host Total Listings against Price Per Night shows a distinct upward shift, proving that institutional managers successfully command higher pricing thresholds than individual property owners.
